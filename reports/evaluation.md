# Evaluation report (Part 3 models)

Generated by `training/evaluation_report.py` from `models/*/manifest.json` and `reports/*.csv`.

## Methodology common to all models

- **Splits**: one file, `data/processed/splits.parquet`, assigns every one of 1.84M properties to train/val/test (70/15/15) **by synthetic customer**, so no property or customer appears in two splits. Historic certificates and sales inherit their property's split.
- **Train** fits the model; **val** is used for early stopping and threshold tuning only; **test** is reported and never used for any choice.
- **Uncertainty**: 95% CIs are bootstraps over *properties* (all rows of a property resampled together), 200 resamples.
- **Features**: `greenhome/ml/features.py`, the same builder at training and inference; only fields the app receives (HomeInput) are used. Hidden simulator factors are never features.
- **Baselines**: every model is compared with simple baselines on the same rows.
- **Determinism**: LightGBM seed 42, deterministic mode.

## Limitations

- M1 is trained on simulated outcomes calibrated to NEED; agreement with simulated targets does not prove real-world accuracy (see the performance-gap table).
- M3 uses region-level location only, so absolute price error is large; the uplift ratio is what the app uses, applied to the user's own value.
- M2 labels reflect assessor software rules as much as home need; heat-pump labels exist only from 2025.
- City-heavy sample (11 local authorities); England and Wales only.
